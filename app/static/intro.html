Lobsta

Three steps, and the first one is a photo

No catalog to import, no recipes to type, no codes to assign. Each restaurant is one small config file, set up once.

  1. 1Photograph the supplier's invoice. A vision model copies what is printed.
  2. 2Plain code checks every line, converts to cost per kg, and compares with your past invoices. It flags overcharges, price jumps and shrinking packs in your language.
  3. 3Tap confirm. Only then is anything saved. Ask the front desk what's left or what to order.
